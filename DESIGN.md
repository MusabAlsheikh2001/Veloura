# Veloura — Design System V2

## Creative territory
**Digital craftsmanship / architectural precision.** A full-stack product engineering studio with a flagship owned platform: cinematic restraint, exacting interface geometry, memorable interactions, credible product storytelling. Avoid startup-template gradients, boutique-like gold headlines, overrounded cards, fake dashboards, and decorative blobs.

## Visual identity
- Canvas: porcelain `#F6F6F3`, obsidian `#101114`, graphite `#1D2026`.
- Brand: velvet burgundy `#6B2037`, rose light `#E7A9B4`; champagne only within the approved logo itself.
- Contrast: warm white on dark `#FAFAF7`, charcoal `#16181B` on light, muted gray `#74777D` for metadata with legible contrast.
- Typography: refined contemporary sans for primary headings and body (Inter/system); limited optional editorial italic for a single emphasis. Tajawal for Arabic with comfortable leading and no Latin-style tracking.
- Component geometry: disciplined 6–14px corners, 1px borders, generous section gutters, no pill-shaped everything.
- Signature: typography-led editorial split; a functional interaction lab whose panels change on selection (not a decorative logo card), project-led case presentation.

## Structure
Global header: actual logo, Home / Services / Work / About / Contact, EN/AR switch, primary contact CTA; small-screen accessible menu.

Home:
1. Dark full-view introduction and a working interactive engineering showcase.
2. Technology practice, separated into Engineering / Product / Growth.
3. Distinctive cinematic owned-product VeloBase section with a demonstrative, interactive *illustrative* multi-workspace overview; never mislabel it a screenshot.
4. Project-led selected client work with authentic identities and links.
5. Service capabilities explorer.
6. Verified tool stack with real SVG logos.
7. Firm process and inquiry CTA.

Dedicated Services / Work / About / Contact pages share the identical header/footer, tokens, and layouts. Legacy SEO routes remain reachable without inventing content.

## Motion and accessibility
- Visible, precise entrance staging plus meaningful persistent interactivity (showcase mode switch, capability explorer, project selection).
- Primarily transform and opacity; no needless long autoplay transitions; no heavy 3D dependencies.
- Keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus, semantic regions, reduced-motion fallback, responsive layout, accessible headings and accurate anchors.
- Arabic is directionally authored, not merely flipped.

## Acceptance evidence
Angular compile, SSR/pre-render, EN/AR, mobile+desktop, navigation between all core routes, contact form validation (no live delivery claim), contrast and reduced motion. Browser screenshot/video verification is required before calling design complete. No self-congratulation without visual evidence.
